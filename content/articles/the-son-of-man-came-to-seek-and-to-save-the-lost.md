--Message on ICU C-WEEK 2025--
  

I want to ask you a question: Have you ever lost something before? 

Maybe you lost your phone, your money, or your ID card. Maybe you lost your confidence before a big test, or lost sleep during final week. Maybe you even lost your first love, who you really love. And at some point, you may have felt like you lost the direction of your life.

Whenever we lose something important, what do we do? We try to seek it, because it matters to us. Similarly, Jesus also came to seek something He lost. As Luke 19:10 proclaims “For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.”

Jesus often called Himself the Son of Man. This title connects to a powerful prophecy in the Old Testament—Daniel saw someone like a Son of Man coming from heaven, given glory, power, and a kingdom that will last forever. 

But don’t worry, I’m not here to give a theology lecture. What I really want to say is this: We are the lost—but Jesus loves us. That’s why Jesus gave up His glory, stepped down from heaven to seek us—as the Son of Man who has deep compassion, who understands every tear we cry, every wound we carry, and every regret we hide; the Son of Man was pierced and nailed to the cross, dying for our sins, carrying our shame; the Son of Man rose again from the grave, defeating death, so that we can rise into new life with Him!

Some people might say, “Well, that’s just a story.” There are so many religions and philosophies in the world—so how do I know that Jesus is really the Lord and Savior? Where’s the evidence?

That’s exactly why I’m here today. Because my life is living proof of this Scripture.

I’m 20 years old now. I met Jesus when I was 14. Since then, God’s blessings in my life have grown more and more abundant, and His calling on my life has become clearer and clearer.  
Six years ago, I didn’t know Jesus since my parents were non Christians. I was deeply trapped in a cramming education system, faced bullying at school, and fell into severe depression disorder. I even had suicidal thoughts. I couldn’t find the meaning of my life.

One day, I felt an unexplainable urge to open the old Bible in my home. I turned to John 14:6 randomly, where Jesus says, “I am the way, the truth, and the life.”  
In that moment, a long-lost peace fell from heaven into my heart. My soul cried out from deep within: “This is the answer to the meaning of my life I’ve been searching for.”  
As a human, I couldn't find God with my own strength. But the Son of Man—Jesus Christ, who loves me—reached out to find me. That’s when I started going to church.

But only three months later, COVID-19 hit. Churches shut down. At that time, I did not have strong faith. I drifted away from God and fell into sin.

Two years later, in March 2022, churches reopened. The youth leaders, sister Lee, in a church shared the full gospel with me, and I truly confessed, repented, and found freedom in Christ. As it says in 2 Timothy 2:21,  
“Therefore, if anyone cleanses himself from what is dishonorable, he will be a vessel for honorable use, set apart as holy, useful to the Master, ready for every good work.”

Around that time, a rural pastor invited me to translate his sermons from Mandarin to Hokkien dialect (Taiwanese). Another small church asked me to help them build a worship team. A few months later, yet another church invited me to be a youth group leader. I ended up serving in three different churches each week. My spiritual life grew quickly. To translate well, I had to read the Bible deeply—I read the New Testament almost ten times. The more I read, the hungrier I became for God’s Word.

At first, my family didn’t understand my faith. But over time, they saw the transformation in me. One by one, my parents believed in Jesus, my grandmother believed, my aunt believed, and even my close friends became Christians.  
During that time, there was one Bible verse that kept echoing in my heart every night: Mark 16:15  
“Go into all the world and proclaim the gospel to all creation.”

I prayed, “Lord, what can I do for You? I’m weak and unworthy.” At that time, I still struggled with bipolar disorder. Even when good things happened, I felt no joy. When depression came, my brain couldn’t concentrate or function. I felt too young, too broken to be used by God.

So I made a covenant in prayer with God: “Lord, if You completely heal me, I will dedicate my whole life to serve You.”  
But God didn’t heal me right away. I wondered if He even really heard my prayer.

Then in 2023, I met a missionary, Rev. Khoo, from YWAM. While praying for me, he saw a vision: I hold a guitar, sing worship songs, and share the gospel with people from different countries—Japan, America, and across East Asia.  
He encouraged me to keep praying and prepare myself to be used by God.

And now, I see this vision coming true. After coming to ICU last year, when I first joined the English Bible Study, a sister in Christ said to me, “You are a missionary here!” I was shocked at first. But slowly, I realized—it was true. God opened doors for me to serve: sharing the gospel to friends, serving at Worship Night, leading Bible studies. I am so grateful that I am able to live in God’s love and to share and reflect God’s love to people around me.

But, Still, I wondered—why hasn’t God healed my mental disorder?

Until this year’s Easter. During worship, I was singing Living Hope. The lyrics said: “Declare the grave has no claim on me.” In that moment, the six years of darkness, depression, and despair suddenly broken and disappeared. The power of Christ’s blood healed me completely. I remembered the covenant I made three years ago. Right then, I prayed to respond to God's calling: “Lord, I give You everything. I will take up my cross and follow You forever.” Immediately, I was filled with the Holy Spirit—like showers of living water, washing me again and again.

Looking back, every step in my life was in God’s Grace. I was lost, but Jesus found me, and called me to be a pastor to proclaim the same message: the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.” 

Dear brothers and sisters, even though we carry wounds, pain, shame, and inner struggles, even though we’ve sinned against God and others—Jesus still loves you. He is the Almighty God who knew you completely, even before you were born. He came to seek and save you—even dying on the cross for you. He wants to rejoice with you when you’re happy, mourn with you when you’re sad, and walk with you every day.

People may misunderstand you, but Jesus knows your heart and loves you unconditionally. His love never changes. I often joke that the food at the ICU cafeteria keeps getting more expensive, the quality sometimes worse, and the rice portions smaller—but Jesus’s love, grace, and salvation are completely free, and they never change. Jesus didn’t come to trap you in religion; He came to build a real and eternal relationship with you. Human relationships often suffer from distance and brokenness because we live on the verge of sympathy—we try to understand each other, but we fall short. But Jesus fully understands you. He bridges the gap between you and God. Through Him, we don’t have to stay at the verge—we can come to God in perfect closeness, with zero distance.

Originally, our sins—pride, envy, anger, lies, lust, idolatry—separate us from God and bring darkness into this world: emotional struggles, brokenness, even war and death. But God’s love is greater than all sin and stronger than death. Though we deserved judgment, God sent His only Son, Jesus Christ, to pay the full price for our sins. When you come to Jesus, your sins are forgiven! His body was broken so that our past, our shame, our old self, and every kind of darkness in our lives can be broken too. Through the power of the cross, we can be set free and receive everlasting hope and eternal life. When you come to Jesus, death has no claim on you!

So how can you receive this gift? It’s simple—and it’s free. Romans 10:9 says: “If you declare with your mouth, ‘Jesus is Lord,’ and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.” This morning, Jesus is knocking on the door of your heart. He wants to give you a new life. No matter what issues you may be facing—financial struggles, broken relationships, mental health challenges, or anything else—Jesus can enter your life and bring restoration. He has promised to give you life, and life in abundance. The moment you open your heart to Him, your journey of heavenly life begins. Whether this is your first time hearing the gospel or not, I invite you: say yes to Jesus. Just believe, and you will experience the amazing grace of God—comfort, forgiveness, healing, freedom, and everlasting life—just as I have.

If you’re ready, stand, place your hand on your heart, close your eyes, and repeat this short prayer after me:  
Dear Lord Jesus, I know that I am the lost. Today, I believe You died for my sins and rose from the dead. I repent of my sins, accept Your love, and invite You into my heart and life. I will trust and follow You as my Lord and Savior forever. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

Now, still with your eyes closed, I want to bless you. Heavenly Father, thank You that You came to seek and save the lost. Thank You that by the power of the cross, sin, darkness, and death no longer have power over us. I pray Your grace and mercy would follow everyone here today. May Your peace, joy, and love surround them. Help us all be rooted in You, love one another, and live like Christ. And may You bless C-week to glorify Your name. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
